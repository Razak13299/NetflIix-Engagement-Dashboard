🎬 Netflix H2 2024 Engagement & Genre Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)

This Power BI project explores Netflix’s official engagement dataset for H2 2024 (July–December)**, analyzing global viewer behavior, content performance, and genre trends across Films and TV Shows.

📌 Project Objectives
- Analyze top-performing content across Netflix Films and TV Shows
- Uncover viewer preferences by genre, release period, and content type
- Present actionable, visually engaging insights using Power BI

📊 Dashboards Included

1️⃣ Netflix H2 2024 Engagement Overview
- Top 10 Films and TV Shows by views and hours watched
- Release Freshness analysis (Fresh Release vs. Old but Trending)
- Global vs Regional availability analysis
- Film vs TV share in engagement

2️⃣ Top 50 Detailed Genre Analytics
- Custom genre segmentation (Drama, Action/Thriller, Sci-Fi/Fantasy, etc.)
- Total Views vs Total Hours Watched by genre
- Franchise vs Non-Franchise viewer split
- Viewer Behavior Timeline and insight tiles

🧠 Features & Techniques Used
Power Query: Data cleaning, column creation, rank-based filters
DAX: Custom measures for total views, hours, YoY growth, simplified genres
Manual genre tagging from IMDb and Netflix for content accuracy
Appended datasets for unified visualizations (TV + Films)
Netflix-themed color palette with interactive slicers, donut charts, KPI cards, and insight boxes


🔍 Insights Uncovered
- 📈 Franchise content made up 76% of Top 50 views
- 🎭 Drama and Action/Thriller were the most dominant genres
- 🕒 “Old but Trending” content accounted for ~49% of total viewing
- 📺 TV shows slightly outperformed Films in audience hours
